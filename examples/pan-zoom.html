Pan & Zoom View

Interactive pan and zoom controls for map navigation.

Controls

  • Mouse wheel: Zoom in/out (centered on cursor)
  • Middle mouse button: Pan the view
  • Shift + Left drag: Alternative pan gesture
  • Touch drag: Pan on mobile devices

Components

  • PanView: Handles panning gestures
  • ZoomView: Handles zoom with configurable minScale

Integration with Maps

These components work seamlessly with OccupancyGridClient, ImageMapClient, and all marker types. Simply add them to your viewer after creating the map client.
