Arrow Visualization

Demonstrates two arrow marker types:

  • ArrowShape (left, blue): Arrow with line shaft and triangular head. White outline, pulses to show animation capability.
  • NavigationArrow (right, orange): Filled triangle arrow. Commonly used to show robot orientation.

Both arrows rotate to demonstrate how orientation is controlled via the rotation property (in degrees, clockwise).

Use Cases

  • Visualizing robot heading from /amcl_pose or /odom
  • Showing goal orientation for navigation
  • Displaying velocity vectors or flow fields
